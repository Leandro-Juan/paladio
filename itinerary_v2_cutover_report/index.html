    3. Realism Checks & Domain Rule Alignment¶

    Audited against itinerary-realism-validator (.agents/skills/itinerary-realism-validator/SKILL.md):

    1. Meal Biology & Spacing:
    2. meals.py and struct_mapper.py enforce strict meal slot windows (Lunch: 11:30–15:00, Dinner: 18:30–22:30).
    3. Removed all synthetic dining fallback mocks in compliance with the fail-fast mandate.
    4. Meal spacing violations = 0 across all 135 scenarios.
    5. Pacing & Daily Load:
    6. Regret-based insertion respects daily active time caps: Leisurely (180–240m), Balanced (240–360m), Intense (360–480m).
    7. Daily load variance dropped from 26,541.0 to 5,731.9 (-78.4%).
    8. Closure Feasibility:
    9. Hungarian matching uses a +10,000 penalty for closed venues on calendar weekdays. Closure violations dropped from 72 in legacy to 0 in v2.
    10. Transit Sanity:
    11. Walking proximity bundling contracts nearby POIs (≤350m) into super-nodes, reducing daily travel time from 61.4 min to 24.4 min.
    12. Zigzag & Monotony Nuance:
    13. The zigzag ratio increased slightly from 1.48 to 1.79 because v2 schedules thematic geographic clusters across distinct days rather than greedy TSP across the whole city.

    5. Known Limitations & Next Steps¶

    1. 1-Day Flight-Shrunk Trips:
    2. When a 1-day itinerary has an afternoon flight arrival and evening departure, mandatory satisfaction drops if the POI time window physically cannot fit in the remaining window. The solver correctly refuses to violate opening hours.
    3. Phase 6 LangGraph Refinement Loop:
    4. A deterministic critic and repair node (planner_critic -> planner_repair) will catch remaining minor gaps (such as under-allocated days when candidate pools are small) prior to final output.
    5. Phase 7 Explainable Output:
    6. Enriches final_itinerary with daily themes, POI selection reason codes, and dropped POI explanations.
